# RESEARCH LOG

One entry per market day, written by the pre-market routine (5:00 AM CT).
The plan format is mandatory.

## Entry template

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Pre-market research

### Market context
<2-3 sentences: futures, VIX, macro calendar, overall tone>

### PDT status
daytrade_count: N — day trades remaining: N — (if 0: automatic HOLD)

### Trade candidate (max 1 — the single best)
- Ticker:
- Catalyst (specific, from today's news):
- Entry: $X.XX
- Stop: $X.XX (−X.X%)
- Target: $X.XX (R:R X.X:1, must be >= 2:1)
- Invalidation (condition that kills the trade BEFORE entry):

### Discarded ideas (1-2, for the weekly review)
- TICKER — why discarded:

### Decision: TRADE / HOLD
<one line of reasoning>

### If PDT-blocked: mental paper trade
<record the setup anyway; daily-summary logs what would have happened>
```
